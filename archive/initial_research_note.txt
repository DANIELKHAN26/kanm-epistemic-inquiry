So what you would update and publish is a new, maintained version of Laputa. A few points to get right:

Rebuild it rather than patch the old code. Write a fresh version in Python (Mesa + NetworkX) or NetLogo from the published equations in Vallinder & Olsson 2014 (§2) and Angere's manual. The 2014 C++ code is old and may not even compile on current systems.
Prove your version matches the original and with more functionalities. Reproduce at least one published Laputa result, for example from Vallinder & Olsson 2014, before adding anything new. Without that check, reviewers will not trust the port.
Licence. The original is GPLv3. Where to publish. A short software paper with archived code, for example in JOSS or JASSS also as easy usable plugin for researchers to use. Then use the port in your own extension: the seeker–helper model with limit-marking and question substitution.
