# Verified source register

Accessed 15 November 2026. Every entry was checked against Crossref, the publisher, arXiv, or an official repository. Preprints and items with partial verification are labelled. "Read" means the full text was consulted for the project; other entries were verified bibliographically and from abstracts.

## Laputa: software, model, and applications

1. **SourceForge official project record.** [Laputa / epistemenet](https://sourceforge.net/projects/epistemenet/). Lists C++, FLTK, GPLv3, last update 2014-06-20, and the `Laputa-1.6-src.zip` download. The 1.6 source and HTML manual were read to specify historical behaviour; no code is included in KANM.
2. Olsson, E. J. (2011). A simulation approach to veritistic social epistemology. *Episteme, 8*(2), 127–143. [doi:10.3366/epi.2011.0012](https://doi.org/10.3366/epi.2011.0012). Read. Defines the V-value (error delta) and gives the Sherlock Holmes log.
3. Olsson, E. J. (2013). A Bayesian simulation model of group deliberation and polarization. In F. Zenker (Ed.), *Bayesian Argumentation: The Practical Side of Probability* (Synthese Library 362, pp. 113–133). Springer. [doi:10.1007/978-94-007-5357-0_6](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-94-007-5357-0_6); [author copy](https://lucris.lub.lu.se/ws/portalfiles/portal/5684445/7758617.pdf). Read. Gives the equations, Communication Independence, and the threshold and liar rules.
4. Vallinder, A., & Olsson, E. J. (2014). Trust and the value of overconfidence: A Bayesian perspective on social network communication. *Synthese, 191*(9), 1991–2007. [doi:10.1007/s11229-013-0375-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-013-0375-0) (online 2013). Read. Gives the trust-update equation and the Fig. 1 setup used in KANM's reproduction attempt.
5. Olsson, E. J., & Vallinder, A. (2013). Norms of assertion and communication in social networks. *Synthese, 190*(13), 2557–2571. [doi:10.1007/s11229-013-0313-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-013-0313-1).
6. Masterton, G., & Olsson, E. J. (2013). Argumentation and belief updating in social networks: A Bayesian approach. In E. Fermé, D. Gabbay & G. Simari (Eds.), *Trends in Belief Revision and Argumentation Dynamics*. College Publications. No DOI; verified from the Lund repository copy. Gives the derivation and the independence assumption "in every C".
7. Angere, S. (2010). *Knowledge in a social network*. Unpublished preprint dated 29 March 2010, Lund University repository. Read. Gives the multi-source update (eq. 1) and worked trust examples. Its publication status is **not verified**.
8. Angere, S., & Olsson, E. J. (2017). Publish late, publish rarely!: Network density and group performance in scientific communication. In T. Boyer-Kassem, C. Mayo-Wilson & M. Weisberg (Eds.), *Scientific Collaboration and Collective Knowledge*. Oxford University Press. [doi:10.1093/oso/9780190680534.003.0002](https://doi.org/10.1093/oso/9780190680534.003.0002). A simulation study of high-certainty reporting and non-recycling ("new evidence") constraints.
9. Hahn, U., Hansen, J. U., & Olsson, E. J. (2020). Truth tracking performance of social networks: How connectivity and clustering can make groups less competent. *Synthese, 197*(4), 1511–1541. [doi:10.1007/s11229-018-01936-6](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-018-01936-6) (online 2018). Read. Appendix B's worked log is used in KANM's published-value tests; findings are conditional on the study design.
10. Hahn, U., Merdes, C., & von Sydow, M. (2024). Knowledge through social networks: Accuracy, error, and polarisation. *PLOS ONE, 19*(1), e0294815. [doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0294815](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0294815). Read. "A reimplementation (in NetLogo …) of Olsson's social network model"; its agents are described as naïve Bayesian.
11. Masterton, G., Olsson, E. J., & Angere, S. (2016). Linking as voting. *Scientometrics, 106*(3), 945–966. [doi:10.1007/s11192-016-1837-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11192-016-1837-1).
12. Olsson, E. J. (2020). Why Bayesian agents polarize. In *The Epistemology of Group Disagreement* (pp. 211–229). Routledge. [doi:10.4324/9780429022500-11](https://doi.org/10.4324/9780429022500-11).
13. Pallavicini, J., Hallsson, B., & Kappel, K. (2021). Polarization in groups of Bayesian agents. *Synthese, 198*(1), 1–55. [doi:10.1007/s11229-018-01978-w](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-018-01978-w). Abstract only; the full text was not read.

## Source reliability, exactness and order effects (prior art for the factorisation analysis)

14. Merdes, C., von Sydow, M., & Hahn, U. (2021). Formal models of source reliability. *Synthese, 198*(Suppl. 23), S5773–S5801. [doi:10.1007/s11229-020-02595-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-020-02595-2). Read. **Key prior art.** States that the Olsson model ignores the emerging correlation between hypothesis and reliability and is order-dependent.
